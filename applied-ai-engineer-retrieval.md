# Applied AI Engineer — retrieval

**NirogiAI (Private) Limited** · Colombo, Sri Lanka (hybrid) or remote within Sri Lanka · Full-time

## The work

Our doctors read a case that our AI has prepared, then decide. The better the context in front of them, the better that decision and the less time it takes.

This role builds the **retrieval side** of that: finding the right material, from the right source, at the right moment, and proving that it helped. Retrieval-augmented generation is the technique; the point is a doctor who is better informed, and who can see where every piece of information came from.

Whatever you build is subject to our clinical governance, and to the rules that govern which data may be used for which purpose. Those rules are agreed before the work starts, not afterwards.

## What you would do

- Build retrieval pipelines end to end: chunking, embedding, indexing, ranking, and the plumbing around them
- Choose and run the storage for it, and keep the cost and latency honest
- Design prompts and context assembly so that a model's answer can be traced back to its sources
- Build the evaluation: test sets, scoring, regression checks, and clinical review of what the system produces
- Instrument everything, so that quality and cost can be watched over time rather than guessed at
- Work with our doctors on what actually helps them, and remove what does not

## What we look for

- Production Python
- Real experience with LLM applications: retrieval, embeddings, prompt design, and the failure modes of each
- A measured approach: you can say how you know a change made things better
- Honesty about uncertainty, which in a clinical setting matters more than flair
- Plain writing, for colleagues who are clinicians rather than engineers

Useful, not required: healthcare data standards, information retrieval beyond embeddings, Sinhala or Tamil language data, experience with audit or regulated environments.

## What we offer

- A clear problem with a measurable answer, in a system that is already carrying patients
- Direct access to the founders and to the doctors who use your work every day
- Compensation discussed openly at the first interview
